This project was a raft built almost exclusively out of foxtons estate agent signs, empty craft ale key kegs and 700c inner tubes, by me (jonah) and assorted friends toward the end of 2017 and early 2018. It housed a stranger who was between homes for a short while before being hoisted out of the canal and crushed by the Canal and Rivers Trust. I was told by a producer recently that Ludic Acid would benefit from having a "body of work" to give a sense of style and direction, and this raft feels like the ur-project of Ludic Acid, embodying ways of working and collective principles that continue to underpin the work we are making.
