Two bodies. One oversized hoodie.

Where does one person end and the other begin? Second Sight is an emerging collaboration between Jonah Russell and H Barton, bringing hand-to-hand acrobatics into the strange, shared space of a single oversized hoodie.

We’re playing with balance, tangled limbs and the moments when two people start to look like something else entirely. Somewhere between dance, acrobatics and a creature finding its feet.

This is an early-stage work in progress, and Second Sight is a working title. These images offer a glimpse into an ongoing process; the form, story and performance are still taking shape.
