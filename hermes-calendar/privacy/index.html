Jonah’s Hermes Calendar

Privacy policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

This policy explains how Jonah Russell’s private, self-hosted Hermes integration accesses and uses Google Calendar information. The integration has one user: Jonah Russell.

Information accessed

The integration requests access only to:

  • read the user’s calendar list and calendar metadata;
  • read calendar events; and
  • create or update events at the user’s request.

It does not request access to Gmail, Drive, Contacts, Docs or Sheets.

How information is used

Calendar information is used to answer the user’s scheduling questions, review commitments and deadlines, and carry out explicit requests to create or change events. Calendar-derived scheduling details may be recorded in the user’s private local planning notes when the user asks Hermes to maintain them.

Storage and processing

OAuth credentials are stored on the user’s private self-hosted Hermes instance. Calendar information is retrieved as needed rather than copied into a separate public service or advertising database.

To respond to the user’s requests, relevant calendar information may be processed by the AI model provider configured for the Hermes instance. It is not sold or used for advertising, and it is not shared with unrelated third parties.

Disclosure and transfer

Google user data is used and transferred only as necessary to provide the user-requested Calendar functionality, maintain security, or comply with applicable law. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Retention, revocation and deletion

The OAuth credentials remain on the private instance until they expire, are revoked or are deleted. The user can revoke access through Google Account permissions and can delete the locally stored credentials at any time. Any scheduling details retained in private planning notes can also be deleted at the user’s request.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests can be sent to jonah@ludicacid.com.
